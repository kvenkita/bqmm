# bqmm: Bayesian Multilevel Quantile Regression

Fits Bayesian mixed-effects (multilevel) quantile regression models
using the asymmetric Laplace working likelihood and Stan, with an
`lme4`-style formula interface, one or several quantiles per call,
optional LKJ-correlated random effects, post-hoc non-crossing
rearrangement, and the Yang, Wang and He (2016) posterior-variance
correction for valid fixed-effect inference.

## References

Yu, K. and Moyeed, R. A. (2001). Bayesian quantile regression.
*Statistics & Probability Letters*, 54(3), 437-447.

Geraci, M. and Bottai, M. (2014). Linear quantile mixed models.
*Statistics and Computing*, 24(3), 461-479.

Yang, Y., Wang, H. J. and He, X. (2016). Posterior inference in Bayesian
quantile regression with asymmetric Laplace likelihood. *International
Statistical Review*, 84(3), 327-344.
